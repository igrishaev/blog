Why not just patch original `str` function, to make it return "nil" instead of ""? The `format` approach is far less intuitive:

All to-be-concatenated values have to be annotated in a single string at first arg, but what these annotations contain is at a separate declared values. It creates two problems:

1) Less flexible for re-ordering

 It doubles the amount of job you have to do now - re-order both placement of `%s` at the first arg and placement of remaining args to `format`.

2) Increases cognitive load by having to hold more entities in brain while trying to focus on a task

All substitutions are under the same `%s` at first string argument, no visual clues of how it all is gonna glue together in one data structure - have to switch attention between first arg and the rest, to combine it together.

There are limits to cognitive capacity of how much human brain is capable of holding simultaneously. All unnecessary actions to be done thus just deteriorate overall ability to focus on more important stuff. Clojure is about doing as much as possible with writing as little code as possible imo.
